FAQ

Digital Marketing Questions
Small Business Owners Ask

What is the best digital marketing strategy for a small business?

+

The best digital marketing strategy for a small business combines SEO for long-term organic traffic, social media for brand awareness and engagement, and paid ads for immediate lead generation — all supported by email marketing to nurture and retain customers. The exact mix depends on your industry, target audience, and budget.

How much should a small business spend on digital marketing?

+

Most small businesses should allocate 5–10% of their gross revenue to marketing. For businesses looking to grow aggressively, 10–15% is recommended. Our plans start at $199/month for management, with ad spend budgets we help you determine based on your goals and competitive landscape.

How quickly can digital marketing generate leads for my small business?

+

Paid ads can generate leads within the first 7–14 days of launching. Social media and email marketing typically show meaningful engagement within 30 days. Content marketing and SEO take 60–90 days for initial results but deliver compounding long-term growth.

Do I need both SEO and digital marketing for my small business?

+

They complement each other perfectly. Digital marketing (paid ads, social media) generates immediate visibility and leads, while SEO builds long-term organic traffic that doesn't require ongoing ad spend. Together, they create a marketing system that works in the short and long term simultaneously.
