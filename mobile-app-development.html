FAQ

Mobile App Development
Questions Answered

How much does it cost to develop a mobile app for a small business?

+

Our small business mobile app packages start at $999 for a Starter App (up to 5 screens, iOS and Android) and $2,499 for a Business App with user accounts, booking or payment integration, and an admin dashboard. Enterprise apps with complex features are priced custom based on scope.

How long does it take to build a mobile app for a small business?

+

Starter apps typically take 4–6 weeks from design to App Store submission. Business apps take 6–10 weeks. Enterprise apps with complex integrations can take 3–6 months. We provide a clear timeline before starting your project.

Do you build for both iPhone (iOS) and Android?

+

Yes — every app package includes both iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Android development at the same price. We use cross-platform development to build both efficiently, so your small business reaches all customers regardless of their device.

Do I need a mobile app if I already have a website?

+

A website and a mobile app serve different purposes. Your website handles discoverability and first impressions. A mobile app provides a direct, ongoing relationship with existing customers — with push notifications, offline access, faster performance, and a home screen presence that keeps your small business top of mind daily.
