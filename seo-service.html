FAQ

Common Questions About
Small Business SEO

How long does SEO take to show results for a small business?

+

Most small businesses begin to see meaningful ranking improvements within 60–90 days. Significant traffic growth typically occurs between months 3–6. SEO is a long-term investment — results compound over time and continue growing even after the initial work is done.

What is local SEO and does my small business need it?

+

Local SEO focuses on ranking your business for location-based searches — like "plumber near me" or "coffee shop in [city]". If your small business serves a specific geographic area, local SEO is essential. It helps you appear in Google Maps, local pack results, and location-specific search queries where buyers are ready to act immediately.

How much does SEO cost for a small business?

+

Our small business SEO plans start at $149/month for local SEO and $299/month for full growth SEO. These are monthly rolling plans — no long-term contracts required. Pricing is transparent with no hidden fees.

Is SEO better than paid ads for small businesses?

+

Both have their place, but SEO provides compounding long-term value. Paid ads stop generating traffic the moment you stop paying. SEO builds organic rankings that continue driving free traffic for months and years. For most small businesses, SEO delivers a higher ROI over 6–12 months compared to ongoing ad spend.
